Welcome to Opperdash.

My name is Elias. I am the chef and founder of Opperdash. I serve culinary, healthy dishes with Mediterranean influences.

I work exclusively with whole foods and pure produce, in step with the seasons. I do this from the food truck or as catering.

My vision.

Opperdash grows out of a passion for health and a love of flavour. I stand for dishes with intense flavours that are also healthy for the body.

What clients say.

Catering for companies.

A company lunch, team event or staff party: I bring the food truck, set up a buffet or cater in the format that suits your event. The base stays the same: flavourful dishes that are also healthy for the body.
