How many guests can the food truck serve?

Up to 250 guests.

What do you need for the food truck?

A 3 by 3 metre pitch and two standard power sockets on a separate circuit.

What is included?

Ceramic tableware, real cutlery, full setup and breakdown, and service from the food truck.

What can we choose?

Choose a main course only, or add appetisers and dessert. The menu follows the season.

Can you provide drinks?

Yes. I can provide homemade lemonade, mocktails and still or sparkling water.
