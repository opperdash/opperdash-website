What I offer.

What does Opperdash offer?

I cook nourishing culinary dishes with Mediterranean influences. You choose from four formats:

  • Food truck: main courses, served efficiently from the truck.
  • Buffet: dishes beautifully presented in serving dishes, where your guests help themselves.
  • Seated catering: a menu of several courses, served at the table.
  • Walking dinner: several small dishes that your guests eat standing up.

Every format can be extended with appetisers, desserts and drinks.

What is the difference between the food truck and a buffet?

With the food truck I plate every dish on site and serve from the truck. With a buffet I present the dishes in beautiful serving dishes and your guests help themselves.

Can you cook in the kitchen at our venue?

Yes. For seated catering and walking dinners I cook on site. I recommend a fully equipped kitchen, with an oven, hob, enough cold storage and work space. No kitchen at your venue? I am happy to think along and look at what is possible together.

Who do you cook for?

For companies, team events, staff parties and private parties.

The food.

What makes your dishes different?

At many events you choose between burgers, fries and pizza, or a light salad. I cook what is missing in between: dishes with intense, deep flavours that are also nourishing.

Does the menu change?

Yes, my menu follows the seasons. I choose produce for taste, origin and season.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan dishes?

Yes. I prepare both vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes, and vegan is possible. Let me know your wishes in your request.

Do you take allergies into account?

Yes. Let me know about allergies at least five working days before the event, together with the final number of guests, and I will take them into account.

Can you serve fish instead of meat?

Yes, fish is possible instead of meat, at an extra cost.

Can you provide drinks?

Yes. I can provide homemade lemonade, mocktails and still or sparkling water.

Practical.

What do you bring?

Ceramic tableware and real cutlery. Tables and chairs can be included in the quote, and serving staff is available on request.

What do you need on site?
  • Food truck: a 3 by 3 metre pitch and two standard power sockets on a separate circuit.
  • Buffet: tables and one standard power socket.
  • Seated catering or walking dinner: I recommend a fully equipped kitchen. If there is none, I am happy to think along.
Where do you work?

I cook from Leuven and come to your venue, anywhere in Belgium. Travel is charged per kilometre, there and back.

Quote and payment.

How do I request a quote?

Use the quote form for catering or the food truck, or contact me by email or phone. I look at your wishes and put the quote together with you.

What is in a quote?

The price per person for the chosen dishes, a fixed start-up fee and travel. All prices exclude VAT.

How does payment work?

On confirmation you pay a deposit of 20% of the total invoice, with a minimum of €500. The deposit is due no later than five working days before the event.

Until when can I change the number of guests?

Please confirm the final number of guests no later than five working days before the event.
