Cases.

A look behind the scenes of some of the events I catered: who I cooked for, what ended up on the plate and how I approached it.

Food truck · Kortenberg · ± 160 guests

Asmodee

For their showroom evening I served lemon-thyme chicken thigh or curried seitan with herby bulgur, roasted pumpkin, mushroom duxelles, red cabbage salad and za’atar yoghurt.

Food truck · Destelbergen · ± 50 guests

Xandres

For their anniversary celebration I served lemon-pepper chicken and curried seitan, with homemade lemonades and peanut chocolate tart. Almost 50 employees ate within 30 minutes and went straight back to work.

Food truck · Leuven · 26 guests

KU Leuven

A thank-you to colleagues at the end of the academic year. Two amuse-bouches, pulled beef with pointed cabbage and apple slaw or grilled peach with burrata, then pink chateaubriand or curried seitan with pearl couscous and ratatouille. With homemade lemonades and peanut chocolate tart.

Food truck · ± 40 guests

3P

Lunch for the whole team: lemon-pepper chicken or seitan curry, with spiced bulgur, grilled courgette, cherry tomatoes with red wine vinegar, a yoghurt and cucumber salad and a lightly spicy hummus dressing.

Food truck · Festival · Brussels

Couleur Café

A festival weekend at the Atomium in Brussels. Chicken thigh or seitan curry with baby potatoes, sweet potato, cucumber, radish, feta and hummus. No beef and no air-freighted produce.

Food truck · Kontich · ± 100 guests

Veritas

Lunch for around 100 employees, in two sittings. Chicken thigh or seitan curry with sweet potato, baby potatoes, green beans, beetroot, feta and hummus.
