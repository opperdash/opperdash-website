Catering &
buffets.

A generous buffet of nourishing, chef-driven dishes with Mediterranean influences. Homemade and ready to serve.

Chef-driven buffet.

Every season we put together a buffet with Mediterranean influences: meat dishes, vegetables prepared in several ways, a base dish and homemade sauces.

We make everything ourselves, never deep-fry and use extra virgin olive oil as our base. The buffet is presented in ceramic dishes and your guests eat from ceramic plates with real cutlery.

We come to your venue and take care of the setup and the clearing.

Three ways to cater.

Buffet

A light or extended buffet, with optional appetisers and desserts. All you need is tables and one power socket.

Seated

We cook in the kitchen at your venue and serve at the table. From one strong main course to a full menu with appetisers, starter and dessert.

Walking dinner

Small cold and warm dishes that come out of the kitchen one after another. Your guests eat standing up. Choose five or seven dishes.

Capacity.

We match our team to the number of guests and the timing you want, so everyone is served on time.

Please confirm the final number of guests and any allergies at least five working days before the event.

Catering FAQ.

What does Opperdash bring?

Ceramic tableware, real cutlery and the buffet presentation in beautiful serving dishes. We set up and clear everything away afterwards. Tables and chairs can be included in the quote.

What do you need for a buffet?

Tables to present the buffet on and one standard power socket.

Can you cook in the kitchen at our venue?

Yes. For seated catering and walking dinners we cook on site. This requires a fully equipped kitchen with an oven, hob, enough cold storage and work space. Serving staff can be added on request.

Do you take allergies into account?

Yes. Let us know about allergies and the final number of guests at least five working days before the event, and we will take them into account.

Does the menu follow the seasons?

Yes. Our menu changes with the season. We choose produce for taste, origin and season.

Can you provide drinks?

Yes. We can provide homemade lemonade, mocktails and still or sparkling water.
