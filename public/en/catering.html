Culinary buffet.

Every season I put together a buffet with Mediterranean influences. The season decides what goes on the table: I cook with vegetables that are at their best at that moment.

I make everything myself and serve the buffet warm or cold. I dress the table completely, with a tablecloth and decoration, and present the dishes in modern serving dishes. Your guests eat from ceramic plates with real cutlery.

I come to your venue and take care of the setup and the clearing.

Three ways to cater.

Buffet

A light or extended buffet, with optional appetisers and desserts. All you need is tables and one power socket.

Seated

I cook in the kitchen at your venue and serve at the table. From one strong main course to a full menu with appetisers, starter and dessert.

Walking dinner

Small cold and warm dishes that come out of the kitchen one after another. Your guests eat standing up. Choose five or seven dishes.

Capacity.

I match my team to the number of guests and the timing you want, so everyone is served on time.

Please confirm the final number of guests and any allergies at least five working days before the event.

Catering FAQ.

What does Opperdash bring?

Ceramic tableware, real cutlery and the buffet presentation in beautiful serving dishes. I set up and clear everything away afterwards. Tables and chairs can be included in the quote.

What do you need for a buffet?

Tables to present the buffet on and one standard power socket.

Can you cook in the kitchen at our venue?

Yes. For seated catering and walking dinners I cook on site. This requires a fully equipped kitchen with an oven, hob, enough cold storage and work space. Serving staff can be added on request.

Do you take allergies into account?

Yes. Let me know about allergies and the final number of guests at least five working days before the event, and I will take them into account.

Does the menu follow the seasons?

Yes. My menu changes with the season. I choose produce for taste, origin and season.

Can you provide drinks?

Yes. I can provide homemade lemonade, mocktails and still or sparkling water.
